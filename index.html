The idea

Twelve versions, one horizon

This is the spine. A Ramanujan-inspired kernel for gravity, on one Zenodo record. Version 2.1 is the latest. These are preprints. Open a line to read what that manuscript did.

The whole record
  • Read v2.1

    Integrating out a bulk scalar in a five-dimensional warped Anti-de Sitter geometry, then Poisson-resumming the Kaluza–Klein tower, produces the kernel as a ratio of Hurwitz zeta values. An environmental screen suppresses the nonlocal boost to about 10^-19 wherever acceleration is high, so the correction at 1 AU stays under 10^-10. For the Milky Way and Andromeda, about 770 kiloparsecs apart, the nonlocal escape speed is about 349.7 km/s against a relative speed of 110 km/s, so the pair stays bound without a dark-matter halo. Bulk vacuum energy shifts the Planck baseline to H0 = 71.28 km/s/Mpc, which the paper reads as a Hubble tension of 1.5 sigma. A dynamical ultraviolet cutoff scales as the cube root of mass and stays inside the LIGO GW150914 ringdown bound. A six-domain stress suite, including a Monte Carlo of the parameters, is reported as fully compliant.

Things that run

The same habit, in software. Three pieces you can open.

01

PujoRoute

An offline atlas of Kolkata’s Durga Puja. Five hundred and four pandals, and a route you can walk.

Circuit Studio plans the circuit. Pandal Passport marks what you have already seen. The calendar, the metro, and emergency contacts stay on the phone. Only the map tiles need a network. Nothing is tracked.

02

CareGuardian

A command desk for a ward: patient, family, doctor, nurse, pharmacist.

Each role has its own desk. The floor flashes when a bed leaves a safe range. Dispatch and medication sit on the same surface. The public build uses demonstration data — the interface is real, the hospital is not.

03

SelfGraph

A private reading of whether what you do matches what you write.

Paste a stretch of your own words. It scores consistency, execution, discipline, contradiction, and clarity, and keeps the history on your machine so the next reading can be set against the last.
